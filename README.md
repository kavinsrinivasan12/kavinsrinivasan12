## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏦 [Bank Loan Management System](https://github.com/kavinsrinivasan12/Bank-Loan-Management-System)
`SQL` `GCP BigQuery` `CTEs` `Window Functions`
- Designed an **8-table relational database** with **~305K records** in BigQuery to unify scattered loan data.
- Wrote CTE, window function and multi-join queries answering **13 real-world business questions** on borrower behavior, loan performance and default patterns.

### 🚴 [Adventure Works Sales Dashboard](https://github.com/kavinsrinivasan12/Adventure-Works-Power-BI)
`Power BI` `DAX` `Power Query` `Star Schema`
- Modeled raw sales, product and customer files into a **star schema** and built a **4-page dashboard** (executive KPIs, sales trends, product & returns, customer behavior) on **$24.9M** of revenue.
- Used **20+ custom DAX measures**, Key Influencers, decomposition trees, anomaly detection, bookmarks and drill-through.
- Uncovered that top-selling **Sport-100 Helmets** had return rates nearly **50% above** the 2.2% healthy average.

### 🛍️ [Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis](https://github.com/kavinsrinivasan12/Customer-Shopping-Behavior-Analysis)
`Python` `SQL` `Power BI`
- Cleaned data in Python, queried spending by age, gender, subscription and discount in SQL, and segmented **3.9K customers** into New, Returning and Loyal.
- Surfaced **233K** in revenue, with discounted purchases at **~43%** of revenue and only **27%** of customers subscribed, signalling room to grow subscriptions.
